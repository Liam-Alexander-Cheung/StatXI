Team Rolling Form

Weighted win rate over a team's last 10 years of matches, weighted by recency and tournament importance.

Head-to-Head Record

Weighted win rate for one team against another, across their full shared match history.

Goal Trend

Weighted average goals scored, conceded, and differential over a team's last 10 years of matches.

Squad Age & Depth

Mean squad age and player counts/proportions per position (GK/DF/MF/FW) for a team's squad at one specific tournament.

Team Chemistry

Club-cohesion of a team's squad at one tournament: biggest single-club and two-club spines, a concentration index (HHI), and how many teammate pairs share a club. Higher = a squad built on one or two club cores rather than scattered across many clubs.
