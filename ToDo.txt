         - DRAWS ARE POISSON'S JOB (investigated 2026-08-05, deferred to here).
           The WDL model almost never predicts draws, but this is NOT a bug to
           "fix" in XGBoost: its draw probabilities are already ~perfectly
           calibrated (mean P(D)=0.233 vs actual draw rate 0.233, tracks the
           diagonal per-bin). It just never picks draw as the argmax because a
           correctly-calibrated P(D) tops out at ~0.389 — even in coin-flip games
           (|P(H)-P(A)|<3%) the actual split is H .336/D .303/A .361, so draw is
           never the plurality. Forcing more draws (class weights / lean-to-draw
           rule) would LOWER accuracy and worsen log-loss — do not do it. Also:
           knockout matches decided on penalties are stored as DRAWS (full-time
           pre-shootout score, e.g. England 1-1 Italy, Argentina 3-3 France), so
           "discard draws for penalty games" is impossible pre-match and wrong
           under the 1X2 target. The real fix = model the score distribution and
           let draws fall out, with the Dixon-Coles low-score correction (which
           exists precisely because independent Poisson under-predicts 0-0/1-1).
           A separate "who advances" (2-way, ~50/50 shootout) target is the home
           for knockout resolution, needed by the Monte Carlo simulator.
